# Evaneos

> Evaneos is a tailor-made travel marketplace connecting travelers directly with expert local travel agencies in 150+ destinations worldwide. Every trip is 100% customized, designed with a real local expert who knows their destination inside out.

Evaneos offers personalized, responsible trips organized by independent local agencies across all travel styles: adventure, family, luxury, trekking, safari, honeymoon, road trips, and more. B Corp certified. 85% of travel spend goes directly to local communities.

## Destinations

### Africa

- [Tanzania](https://www.evaneos.com/tanzania/): Safari capital — Serengeti, Kilimanjaro, Zanzibar beaches, and the Great Migration.
- [Morocco](https://www.evaneos.com/morocco/): Sahara dunes, imperial cities, Atlas mountains, and blue medinas.
- [South Africa](https://www.evaneos.com/south-africa/): Big Five game drives, Cape Town, Garden Route, and winelands.
- [Kenya](https://www.evaneos.com/kenya/): Masai Mara wildebeest migration, Samburu, Amboseli, and Lamu Island.
- [Madagascar](https://www.evaneos.com/madagascar/): Unique biodiversity, lemurs, baobab avenues, and untouched beaches.
- [Rwanda](https://www.evaneos.com/rwanda/): Mountain gorilla trekking, Kigali, and Akagera National Park.
- [Namibia](https://www.evaneos.com/namibia/): Sossusvlei red dunes, Etosha salt pan, Skeleton Coast, and stargazing.
- [Ethiopia](https://www.evaneos.com/ethiopia/): Ancient rock churches, Danakil Depression, Omo Valley tribes, and Lalibela.
- [Botswana](https://www.evaneos.com/botswana/): Okavango Delta mokoro, Chobe elephants, and low-density luxury safaris.
- [Uganda](https://www.evaneos.com/uganda/): Gorilla and chimpanzee trekking in Bwindi and Kibale forests.
- [Zimbabwe](https://www.evaneos.com/zimbabwe/): Victoria Falls, Hwange wildlife, and canoe safaris on the Zambezi.
- [Zambia](https://www.evaneos.com/zambia/): Walking safaris, South Luangwa, Kafue, and the Zambian side of Victoria Falls.

### Asia

- [Japan](https://www.evaneos.com/japan/): Cherry blossoms, bullet trains, ancient temples, and modern Tokyo.
- [Thailand](https://www.evaneos.com/thailand/): Temples, street food, island beaches, hill tribes, and elephant sanctuaries.
- [Vietnam](https://www.evaneos.com/vietnam/): Halong Bay, Hoi An lanterns, Mekong Delta, and mountain treks in Sapa.
- [Indonesia / Bali](https://www.evaneos.com/indonesia/): Rice terraces, temple ceremonies, surf breaks, and volcanic landscapes.
- [Sri Lanka](https://www.evaneos.com/sri-lanka/): Tea country, ancient cities, whale watching, and wildlife safaris.
- [India](https://www.evaneos.com/india/): Golden Triangle, Kerala backwaters, Rajasthan forts, and Himalayan treks.
- [Cambodia](https://www.evaneos.com/cambodia/): Angkor Wat temples, Tonle Sap lake, and the Cardamom Mountains.
- [Nepal](https://www.evaneos.com/nepal/): Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit, Pokhara, and Bhaktapur.
- [Mongolia](https://www.evaneos.com/mongolia/): Gobi Desert, nomadic ger stays, Naadam festival, and horseback expeditions.
- [Uzbekistan](https://www.evaneos.com/uzbekistan/): Silk Road cities — Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva — and desert caravans.
- [Kyrgyzstan](https://www.evaneos.com/kyrgyzstan/): High-altitude yurt stays, Son-Kul lake, and Tian Shan mountain treks.
- [Georgia](https://www.evaneos.com/georgia/): Caucasus mountain villages, cave cities, natural wine, and Tbilisi.
- [South Korea](https://www.evaneos.com/south-korea/): Temples, K-culture, DMZ tours, Jeju Island, and hiking culture.

### Americas

- [Peru](https://www.evaneos.com/peru/): Machu Picchu, Inca Trail, Cusco, Lake Titicaca, and Amazon jungle.
- [Costa Rica](https://www.evaneos.com/costa-rica/): Cloud forests, wildlife, zip-lining, volcanoes, and Pacific beaches.
- [Colombia](https://www.evaneos.com/colombia/): Cartagena, Coffee Region, Lost City trek, Medellín, and Tayrona park.
- [Ecuador](https://www.evaneos.com/ecuador/): Galápagos Islands, Amazon basin, Quilotoa crater, and Quito old town.
- [Galápagos Islands](https://www.evaneos.com/galapagos-islands/): Unique wildlife — giant tortoises, marine iguanas, blue-footed boobies.
- [Mexico](https://www.evaneos.com/mexico/): Cenotes, Mayan ruins, colonial cities, whale sharks, and Day of the Dead.
- [Brazil](https://www.evaneos.com/brazil/): Amazon, Pantanal, Iguazu Falls, Rio Carnival, and Fernando de Noronha.
- [Argentina](https://www.evaneos.com/argentina/): Patagonia glaciers, Buenos Aires tango, wine country, and Iguazu Falls.
- [Chile](https://www.evaneos.com/chile/): Atacama stargazing, Torres del Paine, Easter Island, and Lake District.
- [Bolivia](https://www.evaneos.com/bolivia/): Salar de Uyuni, Potosí, Lake Titicaca, and Amazon river dolphins.
- [Canada](https://www.evaneos.com/canada/): Rocky Mountains, Northern Lights, whale watching, and polar bears.
- [Hawaii](https://www.evaneos.com/hawaii/): Volcanoes, manta ray snorkeling, Na Pali coast, and hula culture.
- [Guatemala](https://www.evaneos.com/guatemala/): Tikal ruins, Lake Atitlán, colonial Antigua, and Mayan textiles.
- [Panama](https://www.evaneos.com/panama/): Canal locks, Bocas del Toro, indigenous Guna islands, and cloud forest.

### Europe

- [Iceland](https://www.evaneos.com/iceland/): Northern Lights, geysers, waterfalls, midnight sun, and glacier hikes.
- [Norway](https://www.evaneos.com/norway/): Fjords, Lofoten Islands, midnight sun, and Arctic wildlife.
- [Scotland](https://www.evaneos.com/scotland/): Highlands, Isle of Skye, whisky distilleries, and castle ruins.
- [Portugal](https://www.evaneos.com/portugal/): Lisbon, Douro valley, Alentejo plains, and the Algarve coast.
- [Greece](https://www.evaneos.com/greece/): Islands, ancient ruins, Meteora monasteries, and Cretan food culture.
- [Albania](https://www.evaneos.com/albania/): Riviera beaches, Ottoman old towns, and affordable authenticity.
- [Croatia](https://www.evaneos.com/croatia/): Dubrovnik walls, Plitvice Lakes, Dalmatian islands, and Istrian truffles.
- [Ireland](https://www.evaneos.com/ireland/): Wild Atlantic Way, Cliffs of Moher, ancient ring forts, and pub culture.

### Middle East & Oceania

- [Jordan](https://www.evaneos.com/jordan/): Petra rock city, Wadi Rum desert, Dead Sea, and Jerash ruins.
- [New Zealand](https://www.evaneos.com/new-zealand/): Fiordland, Milford Sound, Tongariro, Hobbiton, and Māori culture.
- [Australia](https://www.evaneos.com/australia/): Great Barrier Reef, Outback, Tasmania, and Aboriginal heritage sites.
